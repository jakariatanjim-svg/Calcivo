About CalcS

CalcS is a small, open source calculator. It shows the answer while you type, keeps your last results to hand and installs its own updates.

The whole project - source, every release and the issue tracker - lives on GitHub. Read it, build it, fork it: the code is published under the MIT licence.

Built with Capacitor and a hand written expression engine. No ads, no accounts, no tracking, and no third party analytics of any kind.

    Privacy note

    CalcS does not collect anything. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

    Your calculations and your history are stored on this phone only, and they are removed with the app.

    The one time the app goes online is when it checks GitHub for a newer version, and later downloads it. Nothing about you is sent with that request.
